# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

- The six o'clock codebase forecast: a Codebase Weather Report read on the air by a 1940s announcer, in a fixed running order of sign on, warnings, advisories, fair weather and sign off.
- Every line from the report's own evidence; the weather is never recomputed.
- The WREL theme, a valve-radio voice, light crackle and a chime before each storm warning, mixed from one recorded read split at its pauses.
- An offline player with the cathedral radio, a map that lights up the regions being read, captions, The Record and buttons for reading it live.
- TypeScript with no runtime dependencies, 19 tests and CI on Node 22 and 24.
